# INSYTE
INSYTE is an AI-powered meeting intelligence that turns fragmented company signals into a concise, seller-ready brief.


**Summary**
Sales representatives spend significant time gathering information from multiple sources before customer meetings. INSYTE is an AI-powered sales intelligence platform that automatically detects upcoming customer meetings, collects trusted business information from enterprise and public sources, and generates contextual meeting briefs using Generative AI and Agentic AI. The solution reduces manual research effort, improves meeting preparedness, and enables more informed customer conversations.


**Business Need**
Organizations require a faster, standardized approach to customer research. Manual preparation is inconsistent, time-consuming, and often incomplete. INSYTE addresses this by automating research and presenting concise, actionable insights.


**Proposed Solution**
INSYTE integrates with enterprise calendars, identifies external meetings, retrieves company intelligence from financial sources, CRM systems, news providers, and official websites, and generates AI-powered meeting briefs. Insights are delivered through a dashboard, email notifications, and future collaboration channels.


**Expected Business Outcomes**
•	Reduce meeting preparation time by at least 70%.
•	Improve consistency and quality of customer research.
•	Increase sales productivity and meeting confidence.
•	Provide explainable AI through trusted source attribution.


**Business Value Proposition**
INSYTE delivers measurable business value by reducing meeting preparation time, improving the quality and consistency of customer research, and enabling sales representatives to make data-driven decisions. By combining enterprise data with trusted external intelligence through Agentic AI and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), the platform improves productivity, increases meeting readiness, and supports better customer engagement while ensuring explainability and governance.
